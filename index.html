Ringrunner Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Ringrunner is an offline arcade game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Ringrunner has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone so you can pick up where you left off: coins, upgrades and unlocked balls, best scores and records, goals, and settings such as sound and colours. This data never leaves your device through the game. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall Ringrunner.

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include this save data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer of Ringrunner cannot see it.

Permissions

Ringrunner uses one permission, vibrate, for short haptic feedback during play. It is not used for anything else.

Purchases

Ringrunner is a paid app with no in-app purchases. Coins are earned in the game and have no real-world value.

When you buy Ringrunner, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The developer never sees your payment details. Google shares only basic order information with the developer, such as the order number, date and country, which is used for accounting, tax and refunds and for nothing else.

Children

Ringrunner does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
